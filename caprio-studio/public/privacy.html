Privacy

Your information matters.

This page describes how project inquiries are handled on the Caprio Studio website.

Request a project quote ↗

Information you share

When you submit a project inquiry, we ask for your name, email address, service interest, shipping or local coordination preference, timeline, and project details.

How it is used

The information is used to evaluate and reply to your inquiry and, if you proceed, coordinate your order. The website does not include online checkout or collect payment details.

Providers

Our hosting provider processes website requests. Inquiry form delivery may use an email-delivery provider configured by the studio. These providers process data to provide the requested service.

Your choices

Please contact the studio through the inquiry form to ask about the personal details you have supplied. If the form is not yet enabled, please try again after contact details are published.

Updates

This policy may be updated when additional services or tools are added.
